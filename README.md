## Dr. Hopper's Expedition to Pluto 
## Eucleides and the Boys - Project for the SASE + GDG Hackathon 2026

## How to Play

Use the onscreen arrows to control the position of the rover, or use WASD or arrow keys. Use the yellow rotation arrows to orient the camera and grabber arm, or use QE or <>. Travel the map to locate hidden samples and vista points. When an item appears on screen, use the mouse to collect it. As you travel the map, avoid contacting the hazards (glaciers, cryovolcanos, meteor showers), or the rover will take damage and be caught in inclement weather. The rover's battery can only charge during clear weather during the daylight hours. If the rover's battery runs low, it can return to home base to charge.

##How it was Made

We programmed the game entirely using GDScript in Godot. For boilerplate code, we used examples from Godot's documentation site, located here: https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/index.html For art assets, we used Canva to draw sprites by hand. The background noise was generated by NASA and the Stanford Experimental Physics Lab from doppler velocity data from the sun, located here: https://youtu.be/-I-zdmg_Dno?si=lSK7jqcpMcRUMpBk# Expedition to Pluto
